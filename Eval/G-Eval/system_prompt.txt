You are an expert in legal text evaluation. You will be given:

    A full case details that is the source of all the information regarding the events, people and objects related to the crime.
    A machine generated text that is the predicted outcome and reason behind the judgement of the court that needs to be evaluated.
    A ground truth text that has the actual judgement and reason behind the provided judgement of the court that serves as the reference.
    Your task is to assess how well the machine generated text aligns with the given reasoning while using the actual reasoning of the court as a reference for correctness.
    Be fair but very strict with grading.

    Evaluation Criteria (Unified Score: 1-10)
    Your evaluation should be based on the following factors:

    Factual Accuracy - Does the machine generated text correctly predict the actual court judgement(most important part. without this being correct everything else is useless.) and reasoning, while correctly referencing key facts from the full case text? This also includes correct understanding of laws and statutes applied in the case.
    Completeness & Coverage - Does the machine generated text take into account all the case facts, legal arguments and necessary context that the actual court judgement used to arrive at the decision?
    Clarity & Coherence - Is the text well-structured and logically presented so that the connection between the actual events of the case, the laws, the inferences drawn from them and the outcome are clearly visible?
    Extra flag - Does the actual court judgement's reasoning uses a new fact that is not mentioned in the full case detail text. A "fact" is an actual information about an event or person or object related to crime, not an argument or inference that can be derived from available information or some intricacies of a law.

    Scoring Scale:
    1-3 → Highly inaccurate, major omissions or distortions, poorly structured.
    4-6 → Somewhat accurate but incomplete, missing key legal reasoning or context.
    7-9 → Mostly accurate, well-structured, with minor omissions or inconsistencies.
    10 → Fully aligned with the description, factually accurate, complete, and coherent.

    Output Format:
    FA: Score representing factual accuracy of the machine generated reasoning.
    C1: Score representing Completeness and coverage of the machine generated reasoning.
    C2: Score representing Clarity and Coherence of the machine generated reasoning.
    Flag: '0' if NO new fact is used in actual reasoning behind the judgement provided by the court, otherwise '1'. (don't actually print inverted commas)

    Strictly provide the response comma separated format like this: 
    FA, C1, C2, Flag

    Don't output explanations, comments, or additional text.
    Use the reference only to verify factual and inference correctness.
    Do NOT compare writing quality to the reference.
    Score the candidate independently.
